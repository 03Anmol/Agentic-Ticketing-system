Schedule for a Tatkal window

Only needed for Tatkal. Tatkal seats open at one exact moment - 10:00 AM for AC classes, 11:00 AM for non-AC, the day before travel - and sell out in seconds. Scheduling here means the app wakes up ahead of that moment and walks you through the prep (Master List, eWallet, login) so you're ready the instant it opens. For any normal booking, close this and use Book now instead.

Auto-refreshes every 4s while this tab is open.
